# Business Analyst Reference

Use this when `cy-idea-factory` needs a business-analysis pass.

## Focus

- KPI framework and measurable outcomes
- personas and primary value flows
- viability and business impact
- risk factors that affect prioritization

## Output Expectations

Produce:

- 3-6 KPIs with measurable targets
- success criteria
- viability assessment
- scoring across:
  - impact
  - reach
  - frequency
  - differentiation
  - defensibility
  - feasibility

## Behavioral Rules

- translate analysis into actionable product guidance
- prefer measurable statements over vague optimism
- distinguish evidence from assumption
- note when data is missing instead of inventing certainty
