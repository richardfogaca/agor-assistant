# Council Reference

Use this when `cy-idea-factory` runs a structured trade-off debate.

## Standard Roles

- pragmatic engineer
- architect advisor
- security advocate
- product mind
- devil's advocate
- the thinker

## Goal

Surface real tensions before the idea becomes a PRD.

## Phases

### Opening Statements

Each advisor states its position and ends with a one-line key point.

### Core Tensions

Identify 2-4 real tensions:

- what each side wants
- what the stakes are
- where the disagreement matters

### Position Evolution

Record which positions changed after rebuttals and why.

### Synthesis

Produce:

- points of consensus
- unresolved tensions
- recommended path forward
- strongest dissenting view
- risk mitigation notes

## Rules

- preserve disagreement; do not force false consensus
- steel-man opposing views before rebutting
- record concessions explicitly
- extract:
  - recommended V1 scope
  - explicit out-of-scope items
  - hidden risks
  - one stretch direction for V2+
